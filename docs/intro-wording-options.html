Two corrections, and a couple of thoughts

"plain English" takes a capital E — it's a proper noun in there. Worth getting right in something this prominent.

"read the full paper" isn't true of everything. Of your 13 published entries, one is a blog post and one is a public report, and there's a conference poster too. "The full publication" covers all of them, and matches the button on the detail page, which now says View full publication.

"our published work" is doing useful work. It quietly explains why preprints aren't here, which is otherwise a question someone might ask.

On length: your draft as one paragraph is four sentences of unbroken grey text above a table. It's not long in absolute terms, but it's the thing standing between the reader and the rows. Splitting it (2–5) costs nothing and means someone can read the first line and start scanning.

One thing none of these mention: how Helix staff submit their own work. There's a form link in the site config that nothing points at. Worth deciding whether that belongs here, on the internal guide only, or not on the public page at all.
